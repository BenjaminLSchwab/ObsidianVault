Head of Guard. A weathered human in his early forties with close-cropped graying hair and a scar bisecting his left eyebrow. Broad-shouldered and still formidable despite the softness creeping in around his middle. His family swore an oath to the Maximus line three generations back, and he honors it even though Democritus makes him grind his teeth. Keeps a flask in his belt and his hand near his sword. His eyes constantly scan for threats that never come, making him irritable and restless.
