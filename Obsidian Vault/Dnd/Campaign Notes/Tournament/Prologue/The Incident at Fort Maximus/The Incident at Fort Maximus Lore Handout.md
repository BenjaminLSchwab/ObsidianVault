The dwarves of [[Steelbeard Bastion]] are a fiercely independent people, renowned throughout the land for their masterful craftsmanship and their unquenchable thirst for ale. From their ancient forges emerge artifacts of such quality that merchants and warriors alike journey from distant realms to procure them.

<div style="page-break-after: always;"></div>

![[HelmsMantle.png]]
Several months past, your party entered into contract with the dwarves to recover [[Helm's Mantle]], a legendary enchanted shield. This relic was stripped from the fallen body of a great champion, one of the many heroes who marched forth to break the siege of [[Azog the Goblin Lord]]. In exchange for its return, the dwarves pledged a reward befitting such a quest: the enchantment of one piece of each party member's equipment with authentic dwarven runes, a prize of immeasurable value.

<div style="page-break-after: always;"></div>


![[ShieldBargain.png]]
The hunt for this artifact led you through shadowed corners of the world, from bustling port cities to forgotten vaults. Your investigations uncovered a winding trail: the shield had been plundered and sold to pirate merchants operating along the southern coasts, then passed through a succession of unsavory hands before finally coming to rest in the collection of Democritus Maximus, a known connoisseur of dwarven art and antiquities.

<div style="page-break-after: always;"></div>

![[FortMaximus.png]]
Democritus, an elderly human of noble birth though little renown, long ago retired from the political intrigues of the lowlands. He now dwells in his mountain estate, [[Fort Maximus]], where he tends to his extensive collection in isolation.
Our story picks up as your party ascends the winding mountain road toward Fort Maximus, the promise of dwarven enchantment driving you ever upward into the mist-shrouded peaks.
