- Adventure begins with party stuck in [[Fort Maximus]] after a long hard winter froze them in
- they were on a mission from Steelbeard Bastion
	- retrieve a masterwork artifact that had been stolen
	- they had tracked the artifact from a goblin thieving operation, to a merchant who bought it off them for an insanely low price. The merchant had sold it to [[Democritus Maximus]] who considered himself a collector of dwarven art. 
- After finding fort maximus and gaining entry, the party was snowed in
	- Democritus was a good host to them
		- he was very bored up in the mountains and loved listening to their stories
		- he kept stringing them along and promising to give them the artifact if they stayed for a while. Kept coming up with excuses as to why he cant give it just yet
- Session one begins with party listening to Democritus prattle on about dwarven culture in the main dining hall, then dismissing them for the night
	- with the party hearing a knock at their chambers
	- [[Gwimbly]] tells them that something horrible has happened, and they should come quick
	- They find that Democritus has died, sitting on his favorite chair.
	- All the staff know that he had promised them the artifact, and Gwimbly says they should have it now that Democritus has passed
	- Another staff member, an uptight underpaid guy want them to pay a high price for the artifact.
		- there may be a fight over this, as there are a few armed guards in the employment of this manor, and they will side with uptight guy hoping for a cut of the money
- The artifact is tucked away in a trapped treasure room in the manor.
	- while searching, the party can find a map that reveals an abandoned mine nearby that could take them though the mountains without having to brave the frigid pass
- The snows are still quite high, and a trip down the mountain pass would be nearly impossible
- The abandoned mine will be a play on the mines of Moria, complete with dead dwarves and goblins looking to kill them
	- finale will be a collapsing bridge with archers shooting at them, and a giant fire beast chasing them
