There lies the city of Quartum, a once-proud jewel of [[The Holy Shard Empire]], now an “independent city-state”  controlled by the [[Federation of Commerce and Harbors]]. This place is mostly inhabited by humans and half elves, but there are also halflings, gnomes, and the occasional orc. The city thrives on trade and agriculture, but workers grumble about long hours and meager wages while the harbor masters grow fat on profits.

![[OutsideGuildhall.png]]
Quartum's Adventurer's Guild is one of the busiest in the region. Adventurers come from across the land seeking work, connections, and a chance to make their fortune.
The Federation needs muscle. Protecting caravans, dealing with bandits, clearing monsters from trade routes. The contracts are steady.
Refugees from the war-torn Empire territories often pass through, bringing rumors of ancient ruins and lost treasure.
The city sits between multiple power centers: the crumbling Empire to the north, the Larrel Sovereignty forests to the west, and the open seas to the south. Opportunities abound.

![[goblin Camp.png]]
The party accepted a straightforward contract from the guild: a group of goblins had established a camp near the eastern road and was ambushing travelers. Merchants were losing goods, and a few people had been killed. Track down the goblin camp, eliminate the threat, return proof of completion.
The quest went well. The goblins were more numerous than expected, but not well-organized. A few good ambush tactics on your part, some solid fighting, and the camp was destroyed. You found stolen goods, a bit of coin, and returned with goblin ears as proof.
