## Basic Information

**Location:** At a major crossroads about half a day's travel west of Orcod (a frontier town/outpost in Urokgard territory)

**Proprietor:** Halgra Frostborn (more on her below)

**Reputation:** Neutral ground where humans, orcs, and other races can drink without (much) bloodshed. Known for strong ale, hot food, and the kind of clientele who ask no questions.

## The Building

**Exterior:** A squat, sturdy longhouse-style structure built from dark timber and fieldstone. The roof is steeply pitched (northern climate, heavy snow in winter) and thatched with thick layers that have turned gray-brown with age and smoke.

Two massive crossed battle-axes are mounted above the main door—real weapons, notched and blood-stained, supposedly taken from champions who killed each other in a duel over a woman (or a goat, depending on who's telling the story).

Smoke rises constantly from a central chimney. The windows are small, shuttered, with thick oiled leather coverings that glow amber from firelight within. Outside, a hitching post and stable accommodate horses, mules, and the occasional war-beast.

A painted wooden sign creaks in the wind, showing the crossed axes symbol and the name in Common, Orcish, and a few other languages.

**Interior Layout:**

_Main Room (40x50 feet):_

- **The Hearth:** Dominates the center—a massive stone fire pit (8 feet across) with a roaring fire. The smoke rises to vents in the roof. On cold nights, patrons crowd around it. Cast iron pots hang on chains, simmering with stew.
- **Long Tables:** Heavy oak tables with benches, scarred from decades of knives, mugs, and the occasional fight. Arranged in rough rows radiating from the hearth.
- **The Bar:** Along the back wall, rough-hewn timber bar with barrels behind it. Halgra or her staff work here, pouring drinks and serving food.
- **Sleeping Loft:** Above the main room, accessible by ladder, a low-ceilinged space with straw mattresses separated by hanging curtains. Not private, not comfortable, but warm and dry.
- **Private Rooms:** Three small rooms on the ground floor (behind the bar) for wealthier guests. Just a bed, a chest, and a washbasin—but you get a door that locks.

**Atmosphere:** The air is thick with smoke, sweat, and the smell of cooking meat. The fire roars, casting dancing shadows. The noise level is constant—laughter, arguments, dice clattering, mugs slamming on tables, a bard in the corner playing something aggressive on a battered lute.

It's crowded but not uncomfortable. This is a place where hard people come to relax between hard jobs. Violence is tolerated but regulated—Halgra breaks up fights quickly, and everyone knows the rules.

## The Clientele

**The Regulars:**

**Hardened Northern Fishermen**
- One old man, **Bjorn Hookhand** (literally has a hook—fishing accident), holds court near the fire, telling increasingly implausible stories

**Herdsmen & Drovers**

**Sea Raiders (Reformed... Mostly)**

- Mix of human and orc, sometimes half-orc
- Scars, tattoos, missing teeth, fingers, ears
- Wear bits of armor casually—mail shirt over wool tunic, weapons always close
- Trade war stories, brag about raids (often exaggerated)
- Some are retired, some are "between voyages"
- Respect strength, despise weakness, but honor the inn's peace
- **Kormak One-Eye** is the most feared—massive orc with a ruined eye socket, drinks alone, everyone gives him space

**Traders & Merchants**
- A halfling named **Wex Coinfoot** is always trying to sell something—trinkets, "maps to treasure," suspiciously cheap gemstones

**Locals from Orcod**
- An elderly woman, **Marta Blackbread** (the baker), flirts shamelessly with anyone under 60

## The Staff

**Halgra Frostborn - Proprietor & Enforcer:**

_Description:_ Tall, broad-shouldered Orc woman in her 50s. Iron-gray hair in a single thick braid. Face weathered and stern. Wears practical clothes—wool dress, leather apron, sturdy boots. Keeps a cudgel behind the bar and a knife in her boot.

_Background:_ Former mercenary who retired after an injury. walks with a slight limp. Used her savings to buy this inn 20 years ago.

_Personality:_ No-nonsense, fair, protective of her establishment. Will throw out troublemakers personally. Knows everyone's business but keeps confidences. Mother figure to staff and some regulars. Respected even by the hardest customers.

_Quote:_ "You can fight outside. You can fuck upstairs. You can drink yourself blind for all I care."

**Staff:**

- **Dag:** Halgra's son (mid-20s), helps run the bar. Quiet, competent, learning the business. Wants to prove himself.
- **Old Yorrick:** Cook. Missing most of his teeth, makes incredible stew. No one knows his full story, but the scars suggest a violent past. Doesn't talk much.
- **Grimshaw:** Orc stablehand. Takes care of animals, does heavy lifting, breaks up fights when Halgra asks.
## Food & Drink
_Food:_

- **Yorrick's Stew:** Always available, simmering in the hearth. Meat (whatever's available—mutton, pork, mystery), root vegetables, barley. Served with black bread. Hearty, fills you up. 2 copper pieces.
- **Smoked Fish:** Local catch, heavily salted and smoked. Comes with boiled potatoes. 3 copper pieces.
- **Meat Pie:** When available. Flaky crust, savory filling. Actually pretty good. 5 copper pieces.
- **Breakfast:** Porridge, dried fruit, sometimes eggs. 1 copper piece.

_Drink:_

- **House Ale:** Strong, dark, slightly bitter. The standard. 1 copper piece per mug.
- **Halgra's Reserve:** Better ale she brews herself. Smoother, more complex. 3 copper pieces.
- **Mead:** Honey wine, sweet and potent. Northern specialty. 5 copper pieces.
- **Spirits:** Harsh grain alcohol that burns going down. "Clears your head or kills you, either way you stop complaining." 4 copper pieces per shot.
- **Wine:** Rarely available, expensive, usually sour. 8 copper pieces. Merchants drink it to feel sophisticated.

**Lodging:**

- **Common Room (loft):** 5 silver pieces per night. Straw mattress, shared space, not private.
- **Private Room:** 3 gold pieces per night. Bed, door, some privacy.

## The Scene When Grobli Arrives

**Setting the Atmosphere:**

It's evening. The fire roars, casting flickering light across the crowded room. The air is thick with smoke and noise—conversations overlapping, someone laughing too loud, dice clattering, the bard's lute struggling to be heard over the din.


**Grobli's Entrance:**

The door bangs open. Cold air rushes in. Conversations pause as people look toward the interruption.

**Grobli Smackums** stands in the doorway, backlit by fading twilight. He's trying to look important—chest puffed out, hand on his oversized sword hilt—but his helmet is askew and he's clearly nervous.

He surveys the room, trying to project authority. No one is impressed.

_Grobli (clearing throat, voice cracking slightly):_ "AHEM! Attention! I seek brave warriors for a dangerous and... uh... prestigious mission on behalf of Warlord Grommash Bloodtusk!"

**The Room's Reaction:**

- Most people ignore him and return to their drinks
- A few chuckle
- Someone yells: "Get lost, runt!"
- Another: "Grommash hired a _goblin_? Times must be tough!"
- Laughter spreads


_Halgra (approaching the table):_ "You actually taking that job?"

If party confirms:

**Halgra:** "Brave or stupid, I suppose. Either way, you'll want a good meal and rest before heading out. Stay the night, leave at first light. And... [glances at Grobli] ...keep an eye on the goblin. He means well, but he's in over his head. Grommash will blame him if this goes wrong."

She walks away, leaving the party to consider what they've gotten into.
