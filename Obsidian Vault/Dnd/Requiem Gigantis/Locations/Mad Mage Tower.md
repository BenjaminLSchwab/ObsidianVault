## Exterior & Approach

The tower rises from a clearing in the dense northern wilderness, a jagged spire of dark stone. The surrounding forest is unnaturally quiet, and small animals seem to avoid the area entirely.

At the base of the tower stands [[Sir Gareth the Bound]], the cursed knight.
## Ground Floor - Entrance Hall

A circular room with a domed ceiling covered in painted constellations that slowly rotate. The floor is polished black marble with a silver circle inlaid in the center. There is a door on the opposite end of the room.

As they enter the entrance hall, A hologram of [[Lelah The Mad Mage]] appears, playing a pre recorded message: 
"Hello travelers, you have entered the realm of Lelah the great! Literally one of the most powerful wizards in the land. I don't want any scrubs or jerks or door to door salesmen hanging out around here, so beware! Those weak of mind will probably get completely killed on the way up. Those skilled in reverse psychology will be able to ascend and we will meet soon!"

## The Infinite Stairway

The party opens a door and sees a spiral staircase leading up. They climb for what feels like forever, passing the same window, the same crack in the wall, the same scorch mark on the steps. If they turn around and go back, they are immediately at the bottom again.

**The Solution**: Walk _backwards_ up the stairs while looking down

**Hidden Consequence**: If they spend more than 10 minutes in the stairway, they begin to age rapidly (1 year per minute). This reverses once they escape, but it's terrifying in the moment.

## Second Floor - The Library of Mirrors

The walls are lined with mirrors. The room appears to extend infinitely in all directions due to the mirror reflections. A single reading desk sits in the center with the weightlessness tome.

**The Tome**: "Gravitus Nullificus - A Treatise on Temporary Weight Reduction" is open to a page showing a spell diagram. The spell allows one object (up to 500 lbs) to become weightless for 10 minutes. The party can learn this spell or use a scroll version tucked into the book.

**The Trap**: The first player to enter the room will be pulled into the mirror realm and an evil copy of them will come out and fight the other party members.

**Loot**:

- **The Honest Mirror** (cursed): A hand mirror that shows your reflection but also speaks your deepest insecurities aloud whenever you look into it. However, it grants advantage on Insight checks as it also reveals when others are lying.
- Several valuable books on magical theory (50 gp each, 5 total)
- **Boots of Questionable Speed** (cursed): Bonus action, Grant +20ft movement speed, but the wearer must succeed on a DC 12 Dex save whenever they stop moving or fall prone and end their turn. The boots are incredibly comfortable, making them hard to give up.
**Exit**: 
Once the party defeats the evil copy, the original will come back and the door to the next floor will appear.

## Third Floor - The Laboratory of Failed Experiments

A chaotic workspace filled with bubbling beakers, cages containing strange hybrid creatures, and walls covered in frantic calculations and crossed-out theories. The smell is overwhelming - sulfur, and something sickly sweet.

**The Hazard**: Several unstable experiments are on the verge of exploding. Every minute the party spends here, roll 1d6. On a 1-2, an experiment detonates (choose randomly):

- **Alchemical Explosion**: 3d6 fire damage in a 10-foot radius (DC 13 Dex save for half)
- **Conjuration Rupture**: 1d4 mephits are summoned and attack

**Loot**:

- **Potion of Flying** 
- **Cloak of Many Eyes** (cursed): A cloak that sprouts 1d6 functional eyes across its surface. Grants advantage on Perception checks but the wearer is vulnerable to radiant dmg.
- 3 vials of Alchemist's Fire
- A notebook detailing Lelah's attempt to create a "slime familiar" - the entries grow increasingly frustrated
- **Ring of Spell Storing** (contains one 3rd-level spell: Fireball)
- 100 gp in assorted gems

---

## Fourth Floor - Lelah's Sanctum & Rooftop

The top floor opens into Lelah's personal quarters and workspace, surprisingly cozy despite the chaos. Bookshelves line the walls, a comfortable bed sits in one corner, and a small writing desk is covered in papers. A ladder leads to a rooftop hatch.

**In the Room**:

- Claudius the raven is here, perched on a stand. He speaks eloquently and may warn the party that Lelah is "in one of her moods"
- Evidence of Lelah's lonely life: a half-eaten meal, journals filled with magical theories and personal grievances
- A mirror with "You are powerful. You are brilliant. You deserve respect." written in lipstick
- A framed contract with Sir Gareth, it emits magical energy

**The Rooftop**: A flat area with a clear view of Quartum in the distance. The **catapult** sits at the edge, still stained with green slime residue. A journal next to it contains:

- Trajectory calculations
- Timing spell formulas
- A note: "They'll see. They'll ALL see. No more laughing."

Lelah is here, pacing, practicing a speech to justify her actions.
