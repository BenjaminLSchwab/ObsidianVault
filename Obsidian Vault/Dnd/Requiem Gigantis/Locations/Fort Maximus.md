[[Democritus Maximus]]' Retreat high in the [[Titan Steps Range]]

## Visual Description

A striking blend of dwarven architectural tradition and human ambition perched on a mountainside overlook. The exterior features heavy stone walls with carved patterns echoing dwarven craftsmanship but executed with distinctly human elaboration. Inside, polished marble floors reflect light from tall windows, while tapestries depicting the Maximus family history adorn the walls between columns of dark stone. The furniture is grand and ornate, built to fill the generous spaces throughout. The fort commands dramatic views of the surrounding peaks and valleys, visible from nearly every major room.

## Rooms

**Main Entry** A cavernous hall with vaulted ceilings supported by columns. An enormous hearth dominates one wall, the crackles of the low fire echo in the space. Suits of decorative armor line the walls. A grand staircase of dark wood and iron splits left and right to the upper floors.

**Guest Quarters** Six identical rooms along the second-floor east wing, each featuring a four-poster bed with excessive drapery, a writing desk positioned by a mountain-view window, and a private washroom with imported tilework. The furniture is expensive if a bit uncomfortable.

**Servants Quarters** Austere chambers in the fortress's lower level, accessible by a narrow service stairway. Simple cots, shared washing facilities, and small windows that let in minimal light. Functional and forgettable, exactly as much space as needed and not a finger's width more. Gwimbly has claimed a slightly larger room by virtue of seniority and keeps it obsessively neat.

**Kitchen** A large space with a massive iron stove, long preparation tables scarred from use, and walls lined with hanging copper pots and utensils. The head cook, a no-nonsense woman named Marta, runs this domain and suffers no interference from Cornelius or anyone else. Bundles of dried herbs hang from the ceiling beams, and the smell of baking bread almost always fills the air.

**Study** Democritus's private sanctum on the second floor, accessed through heavy double doors of carved oak. Floor-to-ceiling bookshelves line three walls, filled with leather-bound volumes arranged more by color and size than subject. A massive desk of dark walnut dominates the space, its surface cluttered with correspondence, inkwells, and half-finished letters. Behind the desk, a tall window frames the mountain vista. A reading chair and side table occupy one corner. Maps and shipping routes are pinned to a cork board. The room smells of old paper, leather, and the faint mustiness of books.
	(Perception DC 17) Faint marks on the floor and ceiling suggest a section of one of the bookcases can open.

**Vault** Hidden behind a bookcase in Democritus's study, accessible by pulling a specific leather-bound volume (Principles of Mercantile Success). The door is genuine dwarven work - three inches of solid iron with an intricate locking mechanism. Inside: a small stone chamber with reinforced walls. Shelves hold ledgers, deeds, currency, and a few pieces of jewelry. [[Helm's Mantle]] rests on a wooden stand in the center, covered with a velvet cloth, its silver surface gleaming even in dim light.

**Art Gallery** A long hall on the second floor with skylights providing natural illumination. Dwarven paintings, trinkets, children's toys, and sundry artifacts line every wall. There is even a portion of a giant stone pillar preserved in the center of the room, covered in runes. Democritus calls them "important works". The temperature is carefully regulated to protect the art, making it the coldest room in the fortress.

**Smoking Room** A cozy wood-paneled chamber with deep leather chairs arranged around a low table. Walls lined with shelves holding bottles of spirits and tobacco in various forms. The ceiling has stained from years of pipe and cigar smoke, giving it a yellowed patina. A small fireplace keeps the room warm. This is where Democritus conducts his "important business discussions".

**Winter Garden** A greenhouse attached to the southern exposure of the fortress, its glass panels and iron framework a contrast to the stone architecture. Inside, a controlled jungle - exotic plants from warmer climates, flowering vines climbing trellises, fruit trees in large planters. Gravel paths wind between the greenery, and a small fountain provides humidity. A crystal near the center of the greenhouse emits radiant heat.
