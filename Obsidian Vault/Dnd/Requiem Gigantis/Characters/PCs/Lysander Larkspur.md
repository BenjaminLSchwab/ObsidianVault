## Biography
Lysander Larkspur is a human/elf hybrid. His father, [[Gregort Larkspur]], was part of a human traveling band called the [[Glass Slipper Blisters]] and his mother, Mirieth, was an elf studying the history of the elder forests at the local community college. Lysanders parents met at a human tavern called the Lucky Lysander Lounge outside of the elvish territory and his mom fell in love with Gregorts smooth talking and quick fingers on his ceremonial drum that seemed to be a shield as well. Mirieth knew that her father would never approve of a human and she feared that he would not pay her tuition any longer if he discovered their love. The Glass Slipper Blisters stayed in the area due to a good crowd turn out and this gave Gregort and Mirieth ample time to fall in a deep unbreakable love. Some even said Gregort cast a spell on Mirieth. Soon enough Mirieth was pregnant. She continued her studies and hid her baby bump from her father. However one day he discovered her sickening secret. He told her she could continue her studies and keep her unborn baby but she would have to break all contact with the human father. Mirieth agreed to his harsh terms. The only item that Gregort left Lysander was his drum. Lysander grew up not knowing his father, however he would receive letters of grandiose battles and the joys of entertainment. Lysanders mother died of a broken heart before he was the age of fifteen. Now he was sent to his great uncles bow shop to become an apprentice and was banned from music. Lysanders great aunt Linaewen knew music was the only connection to his father and would let him beat his drum in secret. While living with his relatives Lysander discovered his enjoyment of hunting with a small cross bow and the magical abilities he received from his father. Linaewen died when Lysander was twenty seven and he found her journal with multiple entries about Lysanders mothers unruly urge to go and find Gregort. Now with no love left in his life and his great uncles extreme addiction to pixie dust Lysander only saw one option: to find his father and join the Glass Slipper Blisters. So with his fathers drum, a dagger, his crossbow and a satchel full of stories from his father he set out into the great unknown where he met a Bard by the name of Slippery Steven who took him under his wing and taught him how to hone his magical abilities. Slippery Steven was struck down by a halfling shop keep for stealing his golden ring. The halfling seemed to just vanish and Lysander was alone again at the age of thirty four.
