### Backstory
In order to unlock the clarity to observe the collinear nature of time, Bendrankda remained motionless in self inflicted stasis for two years in an isolated cave. While he found paths through past and future, he was blind to the the present. 

A brown mold grew on the rocks above his head, dribling poisonous slime. His body atrophied, his horns deformed and twisted while they grew. The left horn turned and grew back into his skull, slowly worming into his brain.  Mold followed into the wound and infected his insides with acidic slime colonies. 

He achieved divination and woke from his meditation to find that his mind was split and body corroded. He would, without warning, snap into a lust for chaos and pain, drooling green venom. In the times he was himself, he attempted to pull the horn free, but the more he pulled, the more frequent the fits of madness. The tip of horn was keeping the chaos from consuming what was left of him. 

Bendrankda hired a goblin metal smith to cover his horns and the top of his head in steel, then nail it into his skull so the bent horn could never be wrested free.

He now seek an alchemist potion, clerical writ, gnomish surgeon, anything to seal the schism in is psyche and kill the parasite that inhabits it.
