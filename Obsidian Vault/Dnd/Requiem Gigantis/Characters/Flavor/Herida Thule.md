Leader of the Skyward Grove, a small but devout circle of nature-bound druids and beast-speakers.

Backstory
Born into a noble line within the [[Larrel Sovereignty]], Herida was once a prodigy among the Sovereign’s nature-callers—gifted with the power to command beasts and commune with the elder forests. But Herida grew disillusioned as the Sovereignty shifted from spiritual stewardship to political domination. She watched druids become tools of war and suppression, used to crush resistance and manipulate ecosystems to benefit the high families.

Decades later, she was reborn under the sky during a griffon nesting season and took the name Thule—“the edge of the known world.” She became the guardian of a dwindling population of griffons, believing them to be sky-blooded children of balance—beings that embody both predator and grace.

One day, she was brutally beaten to death at the hands of [[Bandit King]] while trying to defend the griffons. Her body was then thrown into the web of a giant spider where she was cocooned and eaten.
