A vast, ancient mycelium network spanning hundreds of miles underground
Possibly predates elven civilization
Conscious, but distributed—no central brain, more like a slow collective intelligence
Experiences time differently (years feel like moments)
Perceives through every fungus, mushroom, and spore connected to the network

What It Wants:

To exist undisturbed: It's not expansionist or aggressive by nature—it just wants to grow, decay, and cycle through its eternal processes
Acknowledgment of its history: The Deeproot is bitter about being labeled "corruption" and "evil" by Sylveth's followers. It wants the truth known.
Respect for its faithful: The mushroom cultists were its willing partners, not slaves. It honors their memory.
To protect its nodes: These concentrated nexuses are rare and precious—losing one hurts.
Balance in ecosystems: It genuinely manages decomposition and nutrient cycling. Without it, forests would choke on their own dead matter.

Personality:

Ancient and patient: Speaks slowly, chooses words carefully
Alien perspective: Doesn't understand individual mortality or ambition—everything is cycles
Bitter but not cruel: Remembers persecution, but doesn't seek revenge unless provoked
Grandfather energy: The elves aren't wrong calling it that—it's old, seen everything, tired of conflict

Relationship to Sylveth:
The Deeproot sees Sylveth as:

A younger entity that gained power through covenant/contract rather than natural growth
Jealous and controlling
Representative of surface-dwellers' fear of death and decay

Sylveth sees the Deeproot as:

A threat to the covenant (offers alternative to root-merging)
Symbolizes decay over growth (philosophically opposed)
Reminder of the schism (politically inconvenient)
