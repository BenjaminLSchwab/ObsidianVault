## Basic Information
Name: Grobli Smackums
Age: Young for a goblin (around 15-16, which is adult but inexperienced)
Race: Goblin
Class: Fighter
Alignment: Lawful Neutral (desperately trying to follow orders and rules to prove himself)
Position: Low-ranking fighter to [[Warlord Grommash Bloodtusk]], volunteers for occasional administrative work to prove his worth.
## Visual Description
Grobli is small even for a goblin—maybe 3'2" on a good day. Green-gray skin with a perpetually nervous expression. His ears are too big for his head and twitch when he's anxious (constantly). Yellow eyes that dart around, always looking for approval or threats.
He wears oversized leather armor. He carries a rusty short sword that's almost comically large for him, dragging slightly when he walks. A dented helmet sits askew on his head, too big, frequently slipping over his eyes.
His posture is all wrong for a warrior—hunched, fidgety, apologetic. He stands at attention when addressing the party but can't quite pull it off, constantly shifting weight foot to foot.
Has a nervous habit of touching his sword hilt for confidence, then immediately looking around to see if anyone noticed him doing it.
## Personality
Desperate for Respect:

Constantly name-drops Grommash: "The Warlord—that's Grommash Bloodtusk, you know him? Big guy, scary—he personally told me..."
Tries to sound tough but his voice cracks when he raises it
"I'm in charge here! Well, not in charge in charge, but the Warlord trusts me with important stuff!"

Incompetent but Earnest:

Gets details wrong: "You'll get, uh, one-third... no wait, two-thirds? Hang on, I wrote it down..."
Pulls out a crumpled, stained piece of paper with instructions
Can't read very well, squints at his own notes, holds them upside down at one point

Insecure and Needy:

Constantly seeks validation: "That makes sense, right? You guys understand? Good plan?"
Takes things personally: "What, you don't trust me? I'm official! I got paperwork!"
Gets emotional: "I'm tryin' here! The Warlord gave me ONE job..."

Tries Too Hard:

Attempts to be intimidating: "And if you try to cheat us, well... ...there'll be consequences. Big ones. From the Warlord."
Mimics what he thinks tough guys sound like: "Yeah, see, here's the thing, the way I see it, strategically speaking..."

Weirdly Loyal:

Genuinely devoted to Grommash despite being treated like garbage
"The Warlord's a great boss! He only threatened to eat me twice this week!"
Takes pride in even menial tasks: "I organized his skull collection by size! Took me three days!"

Socially Awkward:

Doesn't know when to stop talking
Shares too much: "Between you and me, I think the Warlord's favorite lieutenant doesn't like me. He put a snake in my bedroll. But that's just razzing, right? Guys being guys?"
Laughs at his own jokes, no one else does, he keeps laughing anyway

## Background & Motivation
How He Got This Job:
Grobli grew up in the Urokgard's outer territories—not a warrior lineage, more like the goblin equivalent of peasant stock. His father was a cook, his mother worked tanning hides. He desperately wanted to be a warrior, to earn glory, to be somebody.
He joined Grommash's warband two years ago. Has seen exactly one battle—stayed in the rear with supply wagons, mostly just carried water. Got trampled by a panicking goat. The other goblins mock him constantly.
Why He's Here:
When the first party went missing and Grommash decided to hire adventurers, he needed someone expendable to handle the negotiations and logistics. Someone who wouldn't be missed if things went wrong. Someone whose death wouldn't waste an actual warrior.
Grobli volunteered enthusiastically, seeing it as his chance to prove himself. Grommash gave him the job mostly to shut him up.
His Instructions (which he partially understands):

Hire adventurers from the local area
Explain the contract: 1/3 to party, 1/3 to Grommash, 1/3 to the High Urok
They have 5 days to return
Find out what happened to the first party
If adventurers try to cheat or flee, report back immediately

What He Actually Understands:

There's a scary cave
People went in, didn't come out
New people need to go in
Treasure gets split somehow (he's fuzzy on the math)
If he messes this up, Grommash will probably kill him

Grobli's Role in the Adventure
The Initial Meeting:
Grobli approaches the party at a tavern, or wherever adventurers gather:
Clears throat importantly, stands on a chair to be seen
"Ahem! AHEM! Attention! I am Grobli Smackums, official representative of Warlord Grommash Bloodtusk—maybe you heard of him? Big orc guy, very scary, kills people? Yeah, him. Anyway, I got a job opportunity. Very dangerous, very prestigious. and the pay is excellent!"
Pulls out crumpled paper
"Okay so here's the situation, strategically speaking. We found this old tomb thing in the forest we just chopped down, and there's treasure in it probably, but also something killed eight of our guys, which is concerning. So the Warlord in his wisdom decided to hire professionals. That's you!"
Nervous laugh

The Split:
Grobli tries to divide the treasure:

Gets the math wrong multiple times
Uses a small scale that's clearly broken
"Okay so one-third for you, one-third for the Warlord, and one-third for... counts on fingers, gets confused ...somebody. The High Urok! Right!"
If the party is patient and helps him: Deeply grateful, becomes loyal
If the party is mean: Hurt feelings, tries not to cry, probably fails

Gets blamed when things go wrong
Might get killed by Grommash for the party's failures
His death weighs on the party's conscience (if they have one)
Or he survives but becomes bitter, potentially an enemy later


## Key Motivation
He just wants to be taken seriously. He wants to matter. He wants to prove he's not worthless. Every interaction is colored by this desperate need for validation.
The Heart of the Character: Despite being comic relief, Grobli is genuinely tragic. He's trying so hard in a system that doesn't value him. He's loyal to people who don't deserve it.
