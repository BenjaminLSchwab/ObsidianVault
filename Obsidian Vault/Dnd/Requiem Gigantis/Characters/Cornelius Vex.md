## Visual Description

Cornelius Vex embodies the phrase "severe elegance." Rail-thin, he stands six feet tall with rigid posture. His silver hair is slicked back from a high forehead. His face is all sharp angles.
His uniform is a masterwork of tailoring: fine black wool, a crisp white shirt beneath shows exactly one-quarter inch of cuff at each wrist, starched collar. Even his shoes shine like black mirrors.

## Backstory

Cornelius Vex was born into the Maximus family's service. His father served as butler to Democritus's father, and from earliest memory, Cornelius was trained in the exacting standards required to manage a great household. He learned to anticipate needs, maintain perfect discretion, and run a staff with ruthless efficiency.

When the elder Maximus died, Cornelius naturally transitioned to serving [[Democritus Maximus]]. It was not a transition he celebrated.

Where his father had been dignified, measured, and predictable, Democritus was... enthusiastic. Impulsive. Prone to building fortresses in mountains based on aesthetic whims. The younger Maximus had grand visions and the fortune to pursue them, but lacked any sense of practical limitation.

Cornelius could have sought employment elsewhere - his skills were certainly marketable. But the Vex family had served the Maximus family for two generations, and there were standards to maintain. Obligations. Besides, someone had to ensure Democritus didn't bankrupt himself or get killed pursuing his latest obsession.

So Cornelius followed his master to [[Fort Maximus]], managing the impossible logistics of building and staffing a dwarven-styled fortress in the middle of nowhere. He views it as a sacred duty, a martyrdom really, maintaining civilization and order in service to a man who routinely invites armed strangers into their home to pursue dangerous contracts.

His father would understand. Or perhaps he would have had the good sense to retire first.

## Character Traits

**Ruthlessly Efficient** - Cornelius runs Fort Maximus with the precision of a military campaign. Every staff member has their role, every task its schedule, every eventuality its contingency plan.

**Quietly Judgmental** - He has perfected the art of expressing disapproval without words. A slight raise of an eyebrow, a barely perceptible pause, a certain quality of silence - these speak volumes.

**Perfectionist to a Fault** - Nothing is ever quite good enough. Even when things go flawlessly, Cornelius can identify seventeen ways they could have gone better.

**Martyrdom Complex** - Cornelius views his service as a noble sacrifice. He suffers so that others need not, maintains standards in a world that has forgotten them, bears burdens that would crush lesser men.

**Dry Wit** - Cornelius's humor is so understated it often goes unnoticed, delivered with the same flat affect he uses for everything else. Those who catch it are never quite sure if he meant to be funny.

## Example Dialogue

"Very good, sir."

_When a guest violates protocol:_ "I see. You have placed your boots. Upon the dining table. How... novel. I confess in my thirty years of service, I have never witnessed such innovation. Truly, we are blessed to live in an age of such bold creativity. If sir would be so kind as to remove the boots from the table before I am forced to source a new one from the craftsmen."

_When advising Democritus privately:_ "Sir, if I may speak freely - which is to say, with the brutal honesty you pay me handsomely to provide - this is possibly the least advisable decision I have witnessed in a career spanning three decades of inadvisable decisions."
