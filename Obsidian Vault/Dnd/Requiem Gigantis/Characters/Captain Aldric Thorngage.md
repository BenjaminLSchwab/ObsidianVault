## Visual Description

Aldric Thorngage has the look of a man built for violence who has been forced into early retirement. Broad-shouldered and thick-armed, he still cuts an imposing figure despite the softness that has crept in around his middle over the past few years of relative peace. He stands just under six feet tall, with the solid, grounded stance of a career soldier who has weathered too many battles to count. Close-cropped black hair, going gray at the temples A pale scar bisects his left eyebrow, running up into his hairline and down nearly to his cheekbone.

## Backstory

The Thorngage family swore their oath to the Maximus line three generations ago, when Aldric's great-grandfather saved the life of Democritus's great-grandfather during a border skirmish with Urokgard raiders.
Aldric's grandfather served. His father served. When Aldric came of age, he took up the family blade without question. He trained in the Empire's military academies, fought in three separate border campaigns, and earned his scars protecting Maximus interests during the chaotic years of the Empire's decline.

Aldric found himself organizing the security for a construction project in the middle of nowhere. He vetted workers, established patrol routes, planned defensive positions for a fortress that faced no enemies. When [[Fort Maximus]] was completed, he trained a guard force to defend against threats that never came.

Aldric drinks more than he used to. He drills the guards harder than necessary. He keeps himself sharp because the alternative is admitting that his skills are wasting away, that he is nothing more than an expensive decoration in his master's dwarven-themed fantasy.
## Character Traits

**Hypervigilant** - Years of combat have left Aldric unable to relax. He maps every room for exits, evaluates every stranger as a potential threat, and sleeps with his sword within arm's reach.

**Bound by Honor** - The oath is everything. Aldric may resent his circumstances, but he would die before breaking his family's word. This makes him deeply reliable but also deeply frustrated.

**Functional Alcoholic** - The flask is never far from hand. He is not falling-down drunk, but maintaining a steady level of mild intoxication to take the edge off his restlessness.

**Gruff Exterior** - He has little patience for formality or small talk. His communication is direct, often blunt, occasionally bordering on insubordinate (though never quite crossing the line).
