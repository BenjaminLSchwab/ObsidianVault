## Visual Description

Lelah is a human woman in her early thirties with wild, frizzy blonde hair. Her eyes are a striking amber color, and they dart around constantly. She wears robes of deep purple and midnight blue, embroidered with silver runes that shimmer faintly in the light. A silver pendant in the shape of an eye hangs around her neck, and it occasionally blinks independently.

## Backstory

Lelah grew up in Quartum as the daughter of a minor court scribe. From an early age, she showed exceptional talent for magic, teaching herself spells from books her father brought home from the archives. However, she developed a speech impediment, which made her the target of ridicule from her peers and tutors alike.

Despite the mockery, she threw herself into her studies with obsessive dedication, eventually surpassing every wizard in Quartum in raw magical ability. When the position of guild master opened up at the Adventurer's Guild, she saw it as her chance to finally command the respect she deserved. She prepared an elaborate demonstration of her powers, rehearsed her speech dozens of times, and presented herself before the guild council.

They laughed. Not at her abilities, but at her speech, at her wild appearance, at the way she stumbled over certain words. The position went to a less powerful but more charismatic candidate. That night, Lelah retreated to the wilderness and took over a ruined tower, her wounded pride festering into bitter rage.

## Character Traits

**Brilliant but Vindictive**: Lelah is genuinely one of the most talented wizards in the region, capable of feats of magic that would impress even the most jaded archmage. However, years of mockery have twisted her genius into something darker. She sees slights everywhere and responds with disproportionate anger.

**Desperate for Validation**: Beneath her rage, Lelah craves recognition and respect. She wants people to see her as powerful and worthy, not as a joke. This makes her somewhat susceptible to flattery, though she's paranoid enough to suspect manipulation.

**Erratic and Unpredictable**: Her emotions run hot, and she can swing from charming to threatening in moments. She has poor impulse control when angry, which is how she ended up catapulting a slime cube at the guildhall without fully thinking through the consequences.

**Lonely**: Despite her bitterness, she's isolated in her tower and genuinely wants allies and companions. She's just terrible at making friends because she assumes everyone is judging her.

**Powerful but Insecure**: She overcompensates for her insecurities by demonstrating her power at every opportunity, sometimes in ways that are unnecessarily destructive or showy.
